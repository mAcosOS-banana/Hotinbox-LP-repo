## Platform

web

## Users

Urban home cooks: people living in apartments or small homes who have little time, little space and no gardening experience, and who want fresh herbs (temperos) and greens (verduras, microverdes) at home without the work of keeping a garden. The founders' workbook (`knowlegde/`) names the segments: people in the fitness world, parents starting food education with their children, schools and daycares that want a "projeto horta" without the space for one, and sustainability-minded buyers.

## Product Purpose

Hortinbox sells grow-at-home kits: a box that arrives ready to grow, so anyone can harvest fresh, pesticide-free herbs and greens in their own kitchen. The landing page exists to turn a visitor into a WhatsApp sales conversation. Success means a qualified visitor taps through to WhatsApp and buys a kit.

## Positioning

Two claims, both confirmed by the founder as the main advantages:

1. **No skill needed:** the kit comes ready, so the customer grows food without knowing how to garden.
2. **Fits any space:** kits are customizable and adapt to different spaces (and to the customer's routine).

Third differentiator, from the founders' workbook: **support from planting to harvest** for every customer. The current product focuses on **microverdes** (fast and easy to grow). Sustainability (biodegradable materials, no chemicals, the box reused as a biodegradable pot) supports the brand but is secondary.

## Operating Context

- Sales happen only through WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/556596949561`). There is no online checkout, cart or account.
- Two modalities, both with full support: **compra única** (one kit) and **mensalidade** (monthly plan that keeps sending growing supplies). Prices are not public; both are arranged on WhatsApp.
- The brand is from **Tangará da Serra – MT** and delivers to cities in **Mato Grosso** (shipping arranged on WhatsApp).
- Visitors are Brazilian, many on mobile. All copy is in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The founder authorized moving the landing page to React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS (2026-11-25).
- Terms: "kit de cultivo", "horta em caixa", "temperos", "verduras", "microverdes".
- Kit contents (the kit in the photo, per the founders' workbook): 800 g de terra, 2 pacotes de sementes, 1 borrifador, 3 vasos biodegradáveis, caixa com cobertura, 1 manual and 1 mini kit de jardinagem.
- Open decisions: other kit sizes, plant varieties and the customization options themselves aren't documented yet, so don't invent them; the page shows only the kit in the photo and says it can be personalized on WhatsApp.

## Brand Commitments

- The official name is **Hortinbox**. Fix "Hortibox", "Hotinbox" and other variants wherever they appear.
- Tagline in use: "Tudo que você precisa em uma caixa" / "É muito mais do que só cultivo".
- The logo is at `assets/icons/logo-im.svg`.
- Voice: warm, simple and practical, in casual Brazilian Portuguese.

## Product Principles

1. **Zero skill is the promise:** every message should make growing feel effortless and safe for a beginner.
2. **Built for your space:** show that it fits a small apartment, a balcony or a kitchen counter, and avoid images of big gardens.
3. **One clear next step:** every path leads to a WhatsApp conversation. Don't add competing calls to action.
4. **Local and real:** use real kits, real customers and a real delivery area, with nothing invented.
