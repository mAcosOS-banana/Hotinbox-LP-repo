# Landing page Hortinbox (index.html)

**Scope:** the whole landing page, five sections in order: Hero → Quem Somos → Nossos Kits → Propósitos → Chamada para ação, plus footer. **Mode:** Persuade.

**Audience and job:** Mato Grosso visitors on phones (fitness crowd, parents starting food education with kids, schools wanting a horta project, sustainability-minded buyers) who have never planted anything. They must understand in one viewport that the box has everything, that no skill is needed, and tap through to WhatsApp.

**Proof and content:** the real kit photo, the kit contents list from the founders' workbook, both modalities (compra única, mensalidade), origin Tangará da Serra, delivery in Mato Grosso, programs Inova Cerrado and Start&Up (Sebrae). No prices, no founder names, no "40x" figure, no testimonials until supplied.

**Constraints:** React + TypeScript + Tailwind (founder authorized). Keep the logo and the brand greens (#82d85a sprout, #1c7434 forest).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friend sketches, with a marker over the real kit photo, how to grow food from zero: every section is an explained drawing, not a sales block. It refuses the eco-D2C template (split hero with stock leaves, badge row, equal icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: White notebook paper with a faint green graph grid, black marker ink (#1a1a1a), sprout green #82d85a as the only highlighter that means "tap" and the WhatsApp fill, forest #1c7434 for green text, beet magenta #c2185b as the second highlighter for emphasis only. Kalam marker lettering for headings and annotations, Archivo for body. Boxes have wobbly hand-ruled ink borders with a sketched offset outline; numbered comic panels; a sprout-in-a-box mascot ("Brotinho") drawn as line art.

STORY: The visitor sees what is in the box, learns it takes no skill and that someone helps them until harvest, meets a real Mato Grosso startup with a purpose (healthier, more accessible, educational food), chooses one-off kit or monthly plan, and opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: nav strip on a hand-ruled line (logo left, three marker links, WhatsApp button right). Left 5/12: marker headline "Tudo que você precisa em uma caixa." with "caixa" swiped in sprout highlighter, one Archivo lead line, the sprout-green WhatsApp button "Quero meu kit", two hand-ticked checks. Right 7/12: the real kit photo at about 560px, taped to the page, with drawn arrows naming the tools, the sprayer, the seed packets and the soil. Mobile: headline, lead, button, then the annotated photo full width.

FORM: the dealt challenger "Horta Explicada a Marcador" (catalog hand-drawn-zine-explainer), chosen by the user over the assigned direction (position 4 of the grounded list). seed:8e45e1b73697065d. Signature interaction: SVG doodle arrows and highlighter swipes draw on when their panel enters view (stroke-dashoffset, exponential ease-out), already drawn under reduced motion; text is never hidden.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
